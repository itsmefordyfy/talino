## Description

Talino helps you build a tech career in the age of AI. Learn AI and tech skills in short, practical lessons, practise with real projects, and get guidance from Tali or Tala, your personal AI coach, whenever you are stuck.

Whether you are a fresh graduate, a career changer, a freelancer or virtual assistant, an experienced developer, a teacher, or someone who wants to build their own app or website without being a programmer, Talino meets you at your level.

LEARN
• A free starter course, AI Basics for Tech, to begin right away
• Programming Fundamentals: 12 modules on how software works, from problem solving to building a small program, with practice you run in the app
• Data Structures and Algorithms: 72 lessons on arrays, lists, hash tables, trees, graphs, searching and sorting, with visualizers you step through and a task manager to build
• Generative AI: From Fundamentals to Real-World Applications: 67 lessons on how generative AI works, prompting, images, audio and video, your own documents, agents and building a small AI feature, with live prompt labs, a workflow builder and four projects
• Soft Skills for the Modern Workplace: 54 lessons on communication, teamwork, thinking, time, emotions, presenting, clients and your career, practised with AI role-play and a message coach
• Finding International Clients: 52 lessons for freelancers and VAs on finding, approaching and winning clients abroad, practised with a simulated client and a message coach
• Software Architecture with AI: 44 lessons on requirements, architecture styles, data, APIs, cloud, scaling, security and production review, with editable diagrams, a trade-off matrix, decision records and an architecture assistant, ending in a full blueprint and a working proof of concept
• How Large Language Models Work: 77 lessons on what happens inside an AI assistant: tokens, embeddings, attention, how replies are generated, training and alignment, context limits, hallucinations, retrieval, multimodal models and agents, with interactive pictures, a 25-question assessment and a practical challenge. No programming needed
• Time Management and Productivity: 43 lessons on priorities, planning a day, focus, procrastination, simple tools and working with AI without burning out, practised with an Eisenhower matrix, a day planner, a Pomodoro timer and planning exercises scored by clear rules
• Career courses for developers, freelancers and virtual assistants, builders, experienced developers and educators
• Mini-courses on Python for AI, AI engineering, AI-assisted testing, data, no-code automation and selling your app
• An AI-assisted development path: learn to work with AI coding tools, step by step
• Every lesson teaches first, then tests you with a quiz and hands-on practice
• Lessons adapt to your level, from beginner to professional
• Listen mode reads lessons aloud while you follow along

ASK YOUR AI COACH
• Choose Tali or Tala as your coach
• Get help with lessons, quiz questions, exercises, projects, résumés and interviews
• Practise with a mock interview before the real one
• Coaches for career, freelancing and AI engineering
• Free daily chats; AI can make mistakes, so check anything important

BUILD YOUR CAREER
• Track job applications and freelance proposals by stage, with reminders for follow-ups and interviews
• Keep goals and see your progress
• Create a résumé and a portfolio you can share
• Earn a certificate when you finish a course, with a verification link and QR code you can add to LinkedIn

MADE FOR REAL LIFE
• Works offline: your lessons and progress stay on your phone
• No account needed to start
• Earn badges as you learn

Talino is free to download with a free starter course. The other courses and extras are available as in-app purchases.
