## What's New in This Version

New: a "What's new" sheet after every update, and in Settings under About
New: Talino on iPad
• A layout built for the iPad's bigger screen: your course outline beside the lesson, your learning journey beside its summary, and two-column Career and Profile
• Works with Split View and Stage Manager, and the tab bar can turn into a sidebar
New: your AI journey on Home, from Beginner to Expert
• Home is now your learning journey: four levels, every course in the order that builds your skills, drawn as a neural network
• Finish every level to earn the AI Expert certificate, Talino's highest, in its own night-and-gold design
• A redesigned Courses tab: browse by level, category and AI tool, find short courses, and follow featured learning paths
• Smarter search with suggestions for AI tools, topics and courses, plus filters for level, AI tools, Free or Pro and your progress
• Picks that grow with you: improve where your quizzes had room to grow, and get ready for your next level
New: AI-Assisted Development, a seven-course learning path from beginner to advanced
• Introduction to AI for Software Developers: how coding assistants work, where they fail, and how to choose the right tool
• Mastering ChatGPT, Mastering Claude, Mastering Claude Code, Mastering Cursor and Mastering OpenAI Codex: real projects on a real codebase
• Advanced AI-Powered Software Engineering: context engineering, specs, multi-agent work, AI code review, costs, and a capstone app
• An AI tools guide comparing ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and Gemini, checked against each tool's official docs
• "Where should I start?": a short check that tells you which course to begin with
• New badges: one for each course, AI Developer for the whole path, and Capstone shipped
Every lesson now teaches first: an introduction, the lesson, key takeaways, then the quiz, then hands-on practice.
• Choose your coach: Tali or Tala. Each has their own look and their own voice that reads your lessons aloud
• Portfolio, rebuilt: add your own projects with details, links and up to four screenshots each, on a redesigned public page you can share
• Tali or Tala can draft your headline, About text and project write-ups from your own words. You choose what to keep
• Cleaner résumés: a sharper PDF and Word layout, a simpler Create résumé flow, and skills you can add and remove as tags
• A simpler Career tab with your tools in one row, and a new profile card on Me with your progress to a complete profile
• Review my portfolio: Tali or Tala tells you what is missing, suggests better wording you can apply one by one, and recommends the best order for your projects
• Better résumé help: rewrite a description professionally, make it concise or highlight achievements, and get a headline and summary drafted from your own résumé
• Add your profile photo to a résumé or your public portfolio, only when you choose to
