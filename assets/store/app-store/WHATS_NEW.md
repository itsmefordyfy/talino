## What's New in This Version

New: a friendlier welcome that builds your path
• Pick the course you want to learn from the ones that match your answers, with a clear Recommended pick and Free or Pro on each
• A "Great choice" moment for your course, and daily goals with names: Casual, Regular, Serious and more
• The first lesson of every course is free, so you can try any course before you decide. Pro opens the rest
• If you pick a Pro course, see Talino Pro's plans right there, or skip and start with the free first lesson
New: a "What's new" sheet after every update, and in Settings under About
New: Talino on iPad
• A layout built for the iPad's bigger screen: your course outline beside the lesson, your learning journey beside its summary, and two-column Career and Profile
• Works with Split View and Stage Manager, and the tab bar can turn into a sidebar
New: your AI journey on Home, from Beginner to Expert
• Home is now your learning journey: four levels, every course in the order that builds your skills, drawn as a neural network
• Every course is a stop on the journey with your progress on it, and Home always shows where you left off with one tap to Continue the exact lesson
• Home shows the lessons of the course you're learning as a connected timeline, with your next lesson and Continue right there
• Switch course from Home without losing progress in any of them, or see every course on the journey at a glance
• Finish every level to earn the AI Expert certificate, Talino's highest, in its own night-and-gold design
• A redesigned Courses tab: browse by level, category and AI tool, find short courses, and follow featured learning paths
• Smarter search with suggestions for AI tools, topics and courses, plus filters for level, AI tools, Free or Pro and your progress
• Picks that grow with you: improve where your quizzes had room to grow, and get ready for your next level
New: Generative AI: From Fundamentals to Real-World Applications, a beginner course for people who use AI
• 67 lessons in 12 modules: how generative AI works, prompting, images, audio and video, multimodal input, RAG, agents, business use, building a small AI feature and responsible use
• Prompt labs: edit a prompt, run it on a live AI model, compare two prompts, and get a review of your prompt, with a clear label on what is live and what is an illustration
• A workflow builder to design AI workflows with a human review step, plus new interactive visuals for the context window, prompt anatomy, image prompts and the agent loop
• Four projects: a content studio, a knowledge assistant, a business automation and an AI-powered application
New: Soft Skills for the Modern Workplace, a beginner course on working well with people
• 54 lessons in 10 modules: communication, teamwork, critical thinking, time management, emotional intelligence, presenting, adaptability, professionalism, client management and career readiness

• A message coach reviews the emails and updates you write and shows your version beside a suggested improvement, with the reasons

New: Finding International Clients, a beginner course for freelancers and VAs who want clients abroad

New: Software Architecture with AI, a course for developers on designing systems that scale and stay secure

New: How Large Language Models Work, a course on what happens inside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini
