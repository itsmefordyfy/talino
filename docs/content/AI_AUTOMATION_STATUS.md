## Decisions that differ from the brief (all reported)
- **Duration:** the brief says 5 to 7 hours. A standard lesson must be at least 10 minutes (`standard-rules.ts`), so 83 lessons cannot be that short, and the lessons are about 1,000 words of shared teaching each (the brief for writers asked 450 to 700; they ran long for the mandated worked examples). The honest total is about 18.5 hours. `minutes` was not lowered to fit a number.
- **Lesson cap:** `MODULE_COURSE_MAX_LESSONS` raised from 80 to 90 (83 lessons). No other course reaches 80.
- **No tool prices, plan limits or "free" claims:** pricing is taught only as "commonly a limited free tier or a trial, often usage-based, check the tool's own page". Tool vocabulary (Zap, scenario, module, node, item) is hedged as "commonly called, check your tool", and was not checked against current documentation.
- **Hands-on is simulated:** Talino connects to no automation platform. The in-app builder is the rule-checked `workflow-builder` (simulated run, labelled), and the projects are written designs.
- **No role-plays, message tasks or prompt labs:** no new server dependency, so the hosted route needs no redeploy for this course.
- **English only** (owner's standing rule); no Tali or Tala lines written.
