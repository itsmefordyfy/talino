- **Duration:** the brief estimates 4 to 6 hours; the 77 lessons the brief lists add up to **about 16.6 hours** by their own `minutes` (12 to 14 each, the catalog minimum is 10). The lesson list was kept over the time estimate, as in earlier catalog courses; the shared teaching of a lesson averages about 930 words (tables and the details block count), above the 450 to 700 the writers were asked for. Trimming is possible if the owner wants a shorter course.
- **The assessment is five parts, not one page of 25** (a lesson's quiz is capped at five questions by the lesson contract); the learner gets a score per part, not one combined score.
- **Kokoro recordings are not made yet** (queued behind other jobs; a full run is many hours; hosting and upload are the owner's, `scripts/audio/upload-r2.mjs`). The phone's own voice reads lessons until then. If a lesson's text changes after the export, run `export-scripts.cjs` again and delete that lesson's `.timing.json`.
- **No Tagalog** (standing owner rule); new typed `fil` tables copy the English text as a placeholder (listed in `strings-parity` `ENGLISH_ONLY` and `registry-fil` `PENDING_TRANSLATION`).
- **No Tali change:** the coach already knows the lesson and level; a course-specific coach focus was not asked for. No live model call is made by anything in this course (there are no prompt labs).
- **Not seen:** the quiz and practice stages of a lesson, the assessment parts, the workflow-builder activities and the other two new visuals' tapped states on screen (the simulator takes no taps; only the first-load state of each was driven), Android, iPad, dark mode, VoiceOver, a real Free or Pro account, course completion with a real project review (covered by `how-llms-work.course.test.ts` against the app's own progress code, not through the screens).
