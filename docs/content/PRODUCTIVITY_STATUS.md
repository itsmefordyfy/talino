## The tools (`src/features/productivity/`, visuals in `visuals/productivity/`)
- **Eisenhower matrix** (`eisenhower-matrix`): add, sort and remove your own tasks, an optional two-questions helper, kept on the phone. Tap-to-select, no drag and drop.
- **Daily schedule builder** (`daily-schedule-builder`): tasks with estimate and priority, quarter-hour start and length steppers, breaks and buffers, live overlap and outside-the-day flags, work/rest/free summary and plain advice, kept on the phone. An agenda list, not a pixel calendar.
- **Pomodoro focus timer** (`pomodoro-timer`): focus, short and long breaks, pause, resume, skip, reset, custom lengths and rounds, wall-clock based (correct after the app was in the background), finished rounds counted (`focus_sessions`), no notification (stated on screen).
- **AI prompt playground** (`ai-prompt-playground`): seven editable templates (plan my day, plan my week, sort my tasks, break a big task, meeting notes to actions, schedule a project, review my week), what each part is for, ideas to improve, what to check in the answer, Copy, a privacy reminder, a rule-based structure check, and "Ask Tali to try it" (opens the coach with the prompt in the composer). **It is a checklist, not an AI, and says so**; Tali is the live model.
- **Productivity Challenge** = the new activity kind `planning` (XP 12): `prioritize` (sort a fictional workload: 5 of the 15 planning activities) and `schedule` (build a day around fixed commitments and an optional interruption: 10). Scored by **fixed rules, never a model** (`lib/matrix.ts`, `lib/schedule.ts`) on prioritisation, time allocation, realistic planning, handling the interruption and decision making. **Hard failures never pass**: overlapping blocks, work outside the day, a must-do missing, late or far too short, no rest, a stretch of work longer than the day allows, more task time than the day's cap. Free time (slack) and rest are required, so a schedule full to the minute or with no personal time is refused. Every scenario has a reference solution that passes its own rules (`validateActivity` runs it).
- Persistence: tool state in `app_state` under `productivity:` keys, finished focus rounds in `focus_sessions` (migration 10, in "reset all data", **excluded from the cloud backup** like practice sessions); a graded attempt is saved like any activity (attempts, hints, XP once); the half-built plan is kept while the app is open.
